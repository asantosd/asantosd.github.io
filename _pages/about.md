I am a biomedical engineer, MR scientist, and machine learning researcher specializing in **medical imaging**, **magnetic resonance imaging (MRI)**, **brain metabolism**, and **AI-based diagnostic systems**. I am an Assistant Professor and Researcher in AI & Medical Imaging at Tecnológico de Monterrey (2019–present) and a member of Mexico's National System of Researchers (SNI Level I).

I have more than a decade of experience in pulse-sequence development, compressed sensing reconstruction, and deep learning pipelines for clinical imaging, with translational applications in neurology, cardiology, and infectious disease. I have been PI/Co-PI on multidisciplinary projects on MRI/MRS acquisition, AI-based diagnostic support, COVID-19 lesion quantification, radiology NLP, and biomedical imaging systems. I am active in graduate supervision and interdisciplinary collaborations in AI for healthcare.

**Education:** Ph.D. in Biomedical Engineering, McMaster University, Canada (2018) · M.Sc. in Biomedical Engineering, Universidad Autónoma Metropolitana (UAM), Mexico (2011) · B.Eng. in Electrical & Electronic Engineering, UNAM, Mexico (2009).

#### Research areas

Machine/deep learning · MRI/MRS · Brain function & metabolism · Compressed sensing reconstruction · Biomedical signal processing · AI in radiology

#### Current projects

- Automatic radiology reporting for breast screening mammography
- Detection of diagnostic abnormalities in computed tomography using deep learning
- Development of a digital twin of the brain for assessment of Alzheimer's disease
- Prediction of treatment response in prostate cancer using multi-modal imaging
